## Model

The market scenario is $12bn full-adoption annual spend across Nordic-headquartered enterprise groups’ global operations. It is calculated from 5.625m assumed eligible global workers at $100/month ($6.75bn) plus $5.25bn assumed incremental API budgets, not an independently measured market. The evidence section links Eurostat, Statistics Sweden and BCG 2026 research for adoption and the gap between AI use and ROI; these do not validate the model inputs. Sensitivities are $5.6625bn lower-spend and $20.025bn breakout. The full account register retains 34 names; the proposed 31-account Nordic-priority subset is $1,893,412,000, not a strict legal-headquarters classification. Market and portfolio figures overlap.

Year-one Nordic ambition: 160 qualified opportunities × 25% conversion × $2.5m recurring ARR = $100m. Seller starts of 4 / 4 / 6 / 3 / 2 / 2 in months 1–6, with 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% productivity ramp, give 177.5 productive seller-months. At three wins per productive seller-year, capacity is 44.375 wins. One month of seller-start delay reduces capacity to 39.125 wins. These are assumptions, not demonstrated conversion or guaranteed capacity.

The monthly headcount schedule yields $9,231,666.67 in first-year people costs plus $1.5m activation, or $10,731,666.67. Fully staffed annual people cost is $10.88m. Unit costs, support roles and start dates require agreement; product delivery, inference and central overhead are excluded.

## Presentation and account access

Use **Present** in the top bar to open a 17-slide, 16:9 presentation. It requests full screen when the browser permits it; the same presentation also runs inside the window. Use the arrows, Space / Page Down, Home / End or the slide chooser. Escape or Exit returns to reading. Account slides contain at most seven rows so every prior-business account remains readable.

Required-capability rows alternate green and white in both modes. Customer scorecards list 27 prior-business accounts separately from six connection-only accounts. The complete appendix and CSV retain all 34 names, including Hafslund pending relationship confirmation. Nine proposed lighthouse priorities share the same icon in the main scorecards, appendix and presentation.

`account-access.js` stores only user-supplied aggregate access counts. Null means unknown; zero means an explicitly supplied zero; `{min,max}` preserves a stated range. Summary tiles say how many accounts have supplied counts. A person may be both a champion and an economic buyer; totals across roles are not unique people. No individual contact information is published. TAM is modeled annual full-adoption spend; workflow opportunities and deal values require qualification.
