03 / EARN THE NEXT DOLLAR

Value before volume

Validate use cases, willingness to pay, budgets and procurement routes. Exclude SI services, cloud infrastructure charges and partner margins from OpenAI-addressable spend.

Show formulas, sensitivities and revenue definitions

Planning TAM = Σ(groups × eligible workers × workforce multiplier × monthly net spend × 12) + Σ(groups × incremental annual API budget × API multiplier).

Serviceable scenario = workforce spend × seat adoption + API spend × workflow adoption. This is a three-year annualized scenario, not cumulative revenue and not an estimate of OpenAI’s share.

Year-one contracted ARR = qualified opportunities × win rate × (1 − slippage) × annual recurring contract value per win. The ARR model assumes recurring terms of at least 12 months; fixed non-renewing project fees and uncommitted usage are excluded. Minimum API commitments must be separate from included subscription credits. Multi-year total contract value is not counted as one year’s ARR.

Illustrative $2.5m ARR land: 1,000 enabled users × $100 monthly workforce / engineering envelope × 12 = $1.2m, plus $1.3m of separately contracted recurring annual API minimums. This is a deal archetype, not OpenAI pricing or a customer quote; actual product entitlements and included credits must be reconciled.

Base sensitivities: +$10 monthly workforce spend adds $675m to planning TAM; +5 percentage points of seat adoption adds $337.5m to the serviceable scenario; +5 points of win rate adds $20m contracted ARR; 20% close slippage reduces $100m to $80m.

Nordic first-year capacity: 21 sellers, starting in monthly cohorts of 4 / 4 / 6 / 3 / 2 / 2, ramp at an assumed 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% over four months. 177.5 productive seller-months ÷ 12 × 3 wins = 44.375 modeled wins versus 40 required for $100m ARR. One month of hiring delay reduces capacity to 39.125 wins, or $97.81m at $2.5m each. This is a staffing constraint, not a guarantee of demand or customer timing. The model’s hiring-delay control shifts seller starts; it does not change TAM.

Monthly workforce envelope: the $100 base input represents an assumed blended budget for enterprise work, engineering and intensive agent usage, not a quoted seat price. Included entitlements are counted once. Production API budgets of $25m / $5m / $0.5m per strategic / large / mid-market group imply 250m / 50m / 5m billable task-equivalents annually at a modeled $0.10 each. These volumes and unit economics require workload-level validation.

Year-two bridge: $100m opening ARR × 90% gross retention + $100m × 60% expansion on the opening base + 40 new wins × $2.5m = $250m ending ARR. Those retention and expansion assumptions are ambitious targets, not observed performance.
